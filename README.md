## 📋 About this app

Voxrt-asr-android performs speech recognition directly on your Android device. It converts your voice into text in real time without sending data to the internet. The app uses advanced technology to translate sounds into words quickly. It runs locally, which means your privacy remains secure. Your conversations stay on your device and never reach a cloud server.

This technology relies on small, powerful software models. These models process audio signals using the hardware inside your phone. Because the app uses the VoxRT runtime, it works smoothly on most modern Android devices. We designed this to provide fast, reliable results with very low delay.

## ⚙️ How it works

The app listens to your microphone and processes audio frames. It uses a specific neural network architecture called FastConformer. This structure allows the software to recognize language patterns efficiently. The system identifies words while you speak and displays them on your screen immediately.

We optimized the code to use your phone's processor effectively. It uses NEON acceleration to perform calculations faster. This prevents battery drain and keeps the phone responsive while it listens. The entire process happens in the background while you focus on your task.

## 📱 System requirements

This app requires Android 10 or higher. We recommend a device with at least 4GB of RAM to ensure smooth performance. The app needs microphone access to convert your speech to text. You can manage these permissions in your phone settings anytime. 

The software works best when your phone is quiet. Background noise may lower the accuracy of the transcription. Place your phone near your mouth for the best results. You do not need a Wi-Fi connection once the app is installed.

## 🛠️ Frequently asked questions

**Does the app record my audio?**
The app processes audio in real time. It does not save your voice recordings to any storage files or send them to third-party servers. 

**What languages are supported?**
The current version focuses on English speech recognition. Future updates may include support for more languages.

**Why does the battery drain faster when using the app?**
Speech recognition requires constant input from the microphone and active use of the processor. This process consumes more power than static apps. Use the app for shorter intervals to save your battery.

**Can I run this on a tablet?**
Yes, most Android tablets support this software if they meet the version requirements.

**How do I update the application?**
Check the releases page mentioned above for new versions. Download the latest .apk file and install it over the old version. Your settings will persist after the update.

**The app stops working when I close it.**
The app requires an active window to show transcriptions. You can keep it running in the background if your Android version supports split-screen or picture-in-picture mode.

## 💡 Usage tips

1. Speak clearly and at a normal pace.
2. Avoid environments with loud machinery or wind.
3. Keep the microphone distance stable to maintain consistent text output.
4. Use a quiet room if you need perfect accuracy. 
5. Restart the app if it stops responding after long use.

We build this software for reliability. If you experience performance issues, check if other apps rely heavily on your processor. Closing unnecessary background apps helps the speech recognition engine run with less delay. You will find that the app performs better when your phone is not busy with other complex tasks.
